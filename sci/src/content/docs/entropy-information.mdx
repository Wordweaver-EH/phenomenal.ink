# Entropy and Information

In 1948, Claude Shannon introduced a quantity in his theory of communication that looked suspiciously like thermodynamic entropy. He called it "information entropy." But is it just a mathematical analogy, or a deep physical connection?

## Maxwell's Demon

The link goes back to a thought experiment by James Clerk Maxwell. He imagined a demon that could sort fast and slow molecules, apparently violating the Second Law of Thermodynamics. Leo Szilard and later Rolf Landauer resolved this paradox by showing that *information is physical*.

Landauer's Principle states that erasing information has an energetic cost. Maxwell's Demon must measure (gain information) and reset its memory (erase information), paying the thermodynamic bill and preserving the Second Law.

> "Information is physical." — Rolf Landauer
