## Math Equations

**Inline mode**

This is an inline equation: $e = mc^2$

$$8a_2 + 3b^2 = x^2$$ this is another inline equation

**Displayed mode**

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n X_i
$$

Matrix looks nice too!

$$
x + \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 6 & 3 \\ 7 & -1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Numbered equations example**

You can also use `\begin{equation} ... \end{equation}` in case you prefer numbered equations:

$$
\begin{equation} \begin{pmatrix}
a & b \cr
c & d
\end{pmatrix}
+
\begin{pmatrix}
e & f \cr
g & h
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}
$$

$$
\begin{equation} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 & 9 \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 14 \\ 32 \\ 50 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation}
$$

$$
\begin{equation}
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 & 9
\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}
1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} 14 \\ 32 \\ 50
\end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}
$$

## Tables

Tufte-style tables use minimal lines for a clean, academic look.

| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Energy | E | Joule (J) |
| Force | F | Newton (N) |
| Power | P | Watt (W) |
| Momentum | p | kg m/s |
| Angular Momentum | L | kg m²/s |

Tables feature subtle hover highlighting and responsive horizontal scrolling on mobile.

## Image Lightbox

All figures now have a built-in lightbox. Click any figure image to expand it in an overlay with the caption displayed below.

Try clicking on any of the figures above! When expanded, you can:
- Press **Escape** to close
- Click outside the image to dismiss
- Use keyboard navigation

To disable the lightbox on a specific figure, use `noLightbox`:

```mdx
<Figure id="icon" caption="No expand" noLightbox>
  <img src="..." />
</Figure>
```
