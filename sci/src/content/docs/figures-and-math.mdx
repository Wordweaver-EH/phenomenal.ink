## Math Equations

### Inline Math

Einstein's famous equation $E = mc^2$ relates energy and mass. The Euler identity $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ is often called the most beautiful equation in mathematics.

### Block Equations

The Gaussian integral:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

Maxwell's equations in differential form:

$$
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}
$$

$$
\nabla \times \mathbf{B} - \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J}
$$

## Combined Example

Consider the wave equation:

$$
\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \nabla^2 u
$$

<MarginFigure id="wave-viz" caption="Wave propagation visualization.">
  <img src="https://placehold.co/250x180/e8d5b7/a00000?text=Wave" alt="Wave" />
</MarginFigure>

This partial differential equation describes how waves propagate through a medium. The constant $c$ represents the wave speed, and $u$ is the displacement field.
