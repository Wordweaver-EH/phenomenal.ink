There is [something about phosphenes](something-about-phosphenes) and I have been promising people to follow up on the ideas gestured in that piece that followed [my first experiment with hallucination](an-experiment-in-hallucination) or a while now. But writing about science is hard, let alone the science of subjective experience and perception. The difficulty for me is two-fold, the first is of translation and the second is of analysis. Translating a particular experience into something that can be analyzed can be done by two main methods, qualitative and quantitative. Quantitative methods are the mainstay of science and in psychology, in particular, they are often done by scoring on subjective scale items such as on a scale of one to five, how engaged are you reading this essay? Two readers asked this, with different frames of reference, might give the same number for a different experience or the other way around. Subjective experience is, after all, subjective.
