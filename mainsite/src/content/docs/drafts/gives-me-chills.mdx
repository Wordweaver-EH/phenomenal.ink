Something strange is afoot in the playgrounds of the world. Children participate in strange rituals, rituals that were passed down to them from child to child. They enchant rocks to be magnetic,turn fingertips electric and encant strange spells that send shivers through your back:

> "Concentration, concentration," the agent chants,  
> placing a fist on top of the patient's head  
> and slapping it with her other hand.  
> "Crack an egg on your head."  
>  
> She runs her fingers down the patient's head,  
> neck, and back, whispering,  
> "Let the yolk run down,  
> let the yolk run down,  
> let the yolk run down."  
>  
> "Concentration, concentration," she repeats.  
>  
> She pokes the patient between the shoulder blades.  
> "Stab a knife in your back."  
>  
> Her fingers trace down the patient's back  
> as she murmurs,  
> "Let the blood run down,  
> let the blood run down,  
> let the blood run down."
> The patient squeals in delight and fright as he feels chills quiver down his back.  

You might have played this game or [one like it](https://web.archive.org/web/20231004185216/https://forums.welltrainedmind.com/topic/571839-help-me-remember-childrens-rhyme-that-gives-the-chills/) yourself as a child 

Here, notice your hands. Which one do you notice more, is it the one on the left or the one on the right? Notice the difference betweeen each, I wonder which one you feel more connected to, it will be the one where you can notice the movement of air more. Even as you pay attention to that hand, you might find some sensations you hadn't noticed before, perhaps a tingling or difference in temperature. Humour me if you will and take that hand and point your forefinger up to the sky. Focus on the very tip, notice the sensations that you can notice and how where attention goes, energy follows. Take that finger and trace lightly from the bottom of the back of your neck, up along your spine till the bottom of your scalp.  Here, notice your hands. Which one do you notice more, is it the left one or the right one?
